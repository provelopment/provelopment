This page belongs to the **Germany** site in this Foundation reference deployment. Frankfurt is one of the two demonstration locations configured for it, alongside Berlin.

# What a location means here

A location is a physical or service context *inside* a site. It does not create a second page tree: these Frankfurt pages are part of the Germany site, and switching between Berlin, Frankfurt and **All locations** never leaves Germany and never changes the language you are reading.

The time zone and address shown on this page are demonstration data. Replace them with your own location details when you configure Foundation for your business.
