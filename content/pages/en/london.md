This London page demonstrates a **single-language operating region**: it is an
English page bound to the `london` region (`Europe/London`).

Everything in the operational block below — address, phone, email, timezone,
weekly hours, holidays, and directions — comes from the `london` region in
`site.config.json`.

## Demonstration data

All addresses, phones, and emails on this page are **fictional demonstration
data**. Replace them with the real operating details of your business.

From `/en/london` you can switch location to any of the other configured
regions; the location selector always shows every operating location, and each
region resolves to its configured default language when needed.
