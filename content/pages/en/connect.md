title: Connect
---

This page shows the ways this business can be reached. The connection options
below are a **template demonstration** — a real deployment replaces them with
its own methods.

- **Message Us** is the template's configurable contact form (`features.contact`).
- Email, phone, and messenger links (WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber) are deep links
  to demonstration targets.

The footer's Connect section groups **Message Us** with the connection methods
so that connecting with the business is one clear action area.
