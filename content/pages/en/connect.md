## Connect with the Provelopment Foundation

The options on this page demonstrate the Foundation's configurable
**connection methods**. Because this site is a template demonstration, the
methods below are placeholders — a real deployment replaces them with its
own destinations and removes the demonstration labels.

### How the Connect surface is configured

The connection options are configuration-driven (`connect.methods` in
`site.config.json`). Each method has an id, a label, and a destination:

- **Message Us** — the template's configurable contact form, enabled through
  `features.contact` and wired to the configured inquiry endpoint.
- **Email, Phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber** — example deep links marked as
  demonstration-only (`demoOnly: true`) in the configuration, so a visitor
  always knows they are template placeholders.

The footer's Connect section groups these methods into one clear action area,
so connecting with the business is a single step.

### The demonstration boundary

The Provelopment Foundation is a display and demonstration shell: none of the
options on this page are connected to a live operational service today, and
each is explicitly marked **Demo**. Sites built from this template configure
their own methods and remove the demonstration labels.

The eventual operational contact model belongs to **Provelopment.com**, the
operational parent/business site developed from this Foundation.
