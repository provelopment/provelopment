title: Connect

This page shows the ways this business can be reached. The connection options
below are a **template demonstration** — a real deployment replaces them with
its own methods.

- The **message form** is the template's configurable contact form
  (`features.contact`).
- Email, phone, and messenger links are deep links to demonstration targets.

The footer groups **Contact** under **Connect**, making connection options a
first-class part of the site.
