This Montréal page demonstrates a **multi-language operating region**: with
English and French pages it shows that one location can support several
languages while staying one operating context (`America/Toronto`).

Everything in the operational block below — address, phone, email, timezone,
weekly hours, holidays, and directions — comes from the `montreal` region in
`site.config.json`.

## Demonstration data

All addresses, phones, and emails on this page are **fictional demonstration
data**. Replace them with the real operating details of your business.

`/fr/montreal` presents the same region through a French page; use the Language
selector to switch while keeping the location.
