This Toronto page demonstrates a **regionalized page**: it is an English page
bound to the `toronto` operating region (`America/Toronto`).

Everything in the operational block below — address, phone, email, timezone,
weekly hours, holidays, open/closed status, and directions — comes from the
`toronto` region in `site.config.json`, **not** from any global or other-region
identity.
