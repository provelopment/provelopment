# platform-marks-withheld — Provelopment Foundation

> **Status:** deliberate **withholds**. A withheld mark is a successful outcome of
> the admission gate: the platform keeps its authoritative **text** connectivity
> label, which the runtime already renders correctly, and ships without artwork
> until the outstanding question is answered.

## X — WITHHELD

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | X (X Corp.) |
| Status | **WITHHELD** — no `x.svg` parked |
| Official source checked | X Brand Toolkit <https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit>, its `x-logo.zip` (`logo.svg`, `logo-black.png`, `logo-white.png`) and the linked **X Brand Guidelines** PDF <https://about.x.com/content/dam/about-twitter/x/brand-toolkit/x-brand-guidelines.pdf> (reviewed 14 Sep 2026) |
| Reason withheld | The official guidelines limit use: "**You may use the X brands solely for the purposes expressly authorized by X.** Strict compliance with these Guidelines is required at all times". The mechanism the guidelines authorize for showing a presence is the **username/handle lockup** — "We use the logo pairing lockups to clearly show an account is on X" — not a bare 16 px glyph in a labelled list. The published geometric rules then conflict with the runtime slot: "Clearspace: The empty space around the logo should be at minimum equal width left, right, top, bottom" (i.e. ≈ 16 px at a 16 px mark, against the runtime's 6.4 px icon→label gap), and "Logo should be white on black background or black on white background" (neither the light `#ffffff` nor the dark `#0f172a` Foundation surface is black/white). No `@handle` exists, so the authorized lockup cannot be used either. |
| What would be needed before admission | Either (a) written confirmation from X (the guidelines say "please reach out to your X brand partner if you are looking for something that isn't specifically covered here") that a small, text-labelled connectivity indicator is an authorized purpose, together with the clear-space/background treatment X expects at that size; or (b) an owner decision to use the authorized **handle lockup** instead (which implies a real X account and a wider lockup, not a 16 px mark). Until then the item stays a text link — which the runtime already renders correctly. |
| Notes | The retired Twitter bird was **not** used, and no X glyph was drawn from scratch. Assets exist (`logo.svg` is white-only) but were deliberately not parked. |

## Slack — WITHHELD

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Slack (Slack Technologies, LLC, a Salesforce company) |
| Status | **WITHHELD** — no `slack.svg` parked |
| Official source checked | Slack Brand Terms <https://slack.com/terms-of-service/slack-brand>, Slack Media Kit <https://slack.com/media-kit> (its "Logos" link targets <https://brand.slackhq.com/>), and the Slack Brand Center <https://brand.slackhq.com/> (reviewed 14 Sep 2026) |
| Reason withheld | The published terms grant only a **limited licence** and expressly condition it on the brand guidelines: "Any use of Slack brand assets must conform to these guidelines… Slack is the owner of all rights in the Marks and **reserves all rights save the limited license granted here**", with "Contact us (at help.slack.com) to request use of brand assets in a manner inconsistent with the guidelines". The detailed **logo usage guidelines that define conformance are not available**: `brand.slackhq.com/logo` (the URL the terms cite) belongs to the Brand Center, which currently serves "Slack brand center: **Under construction** 🚧" — so the specific clear-space/colour/permitted-context rules for a small connectivity indicator could not be read. The detailed rules that *are* published are restrictive in this context ("For the horizontal logo, the clear space around it should always be greater than or equal to the length of one pill shape in the octothorpe"; "don't modify the marks or use them in a confusing way, including suggesting sponsorship or endorsement by Slack"), and a Slack mark also has **no retrievable official asset** while the Brand Center is unavailable. A Slack mark is also the one item here whose use is inherently ambiguous — a footer icon implies a public workspace invite, which Slack's terms gate. |
| What would be needed before admission | Email/case through `feedback@slack.com` or `help.slack.com` seeking brand-use confirmation for a small, text-labelled link to a Foundation Slack workspace (with a mockup), **plus** a retrievable official asset (Slack logo pack) once it is published. Record the outcome either way. |
| Notes | Recorded status per the brief: **SLACK — WITHHELD PENDING PERMISSION / BRAND-USE CONFIRMATION.** Merely finding a Slack logo SVG on the web was never a basis for admission, and no third-party Slack logo was used. |

## Mastodon — WITHHELD

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Mastodon (Mastodon GmbH) |
| Status | **WITHHELD** — no `mastodon.svg` parked |
| Official source checked | Mastodon Brand Toolkit <https://joinmastodon.org/branding> (official SVGs at `joinmastodon.org/logos/logo-black.svg`, `logo-white.svg`, `logo-purple.svg`, …) and the Mastodon **Trade Mark Policy**, "Last updated August 12, 2026", <https://joinmastodon.org/trademark> (reviewed 14 Sep 2026) |
| Reason withheld | Two independent published blockers. (1) **Trademark**: "The Mastodon name and logos are trade marks of Mastodon GmbH… While the software we create is available under a free and open source software license, **the copyright license does not include an implied right or license to use our trade marks**", "Only use the Mastodon marks to accurately identify those goods or services that are built using the Mastodon… software", and anything outside the policy's enumerated cases needs "express written permission" (requests via `trademark@joinmastodon.org`, deemed denied if not approved within ten business days). The social-media section adds "Mastodon logos cannot be used in a way that might suggest affiliation with or endorsement by Mastodon". (2) **Geometry**: the same official toolkit mandates "**At least 36px on all sides please**" of clear space, and reserves the small "Mark only" form for cases "when the Mastodon brand has been clearly established in the design" with room for "appropriate clear space". A 16 px inline mark in a mixed connectivity list cannot satisfy 36 px of clear space on all sides. |
| What would be needed before admission | A written response from `trademark@joinmastodon.org` covering (a) a small connectivity indicator linking to a Foundation Mastodon presence and (b) how the 36 px clear-space rule is expected to apply at 16 px — or an owner decision to keep Mastodon as a text link. Inferring permission from Mastodon's **software** licence was explicitly rejected. |
| Notes | No community-drawn Mastodon logo was substituted (that would have been the easiest — and wrong — shortcut). |

## Viber — WITHHELD

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Rakuten Viber (Viber Media S.à r.l.) |
| Status | **WITHHELD** — no `viber.svg` parked |
| Official source checked | Viber Brand Center <https://www.viber.com/en/media-kit/> and its published downloads — `Rakuten-Viber-Logos-2026.zip` and `Social-Icons_Viber-2019.zip` (both fetched and inspected, reviewed 14 Sep 2026) |
| Reason withheld | The Brand Center's own social/connectivity guidance is **badge-based, not mark-based**: "Social icons — Direct people to your Viber Community or Public Account with the **Viber badge**. When adding the Viber badge to your materials: Use the Viber badge as provided. **Make sure the Viber badge is large enough that all of the text is legible.** Make the Viber badge the same size or larger than other application store badges. Don't alter the badge. Don't use the Viber badge for any purpose other than to promote content available on Viber." A badge carries text; at 16 × 16 CSS px its text cannot be legible, and shrinking it until the text collapses would violate the published rule. The 2026 logo pack **does** contain compact icon vectors (`Rakuten Viber Icon/SVG/Viber Icon Gradient.svg`, `Viber Icon Flat Purple.svg`, …), but the Brand Center does **not** authorize that compact icon for a social/connectivity indicator — the only social mechanism it publishes is the badge — and the 2019 `Social-Icons` pack is badge artwork (text baked in, per-language). |
| What would be needed before admission | Confirmation from Viber (`media@viber.com`, the contact the Brand Center gives) that the official compact **icon** (not the badge) may be used as a small, text-labelled link to a Viber Community/Public Account, together with any minimum-size/clear-space rule for that icon. Alternatively an owner decision to accept the badge asset in a larger slot (not the 16 px connectivity slot). |
| Notes | Recorded exactly as the brief anticipated: the badge is not automatically a 16 px icon, and no official badge was shrunk into illegibility. |

## YouTube — WITHHELD

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | YouTube (Google LLC) |
| Status | **WITHHELD** — no `youtube.svg` parked |
| Official source checked | YouTube Brand Resources <https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/resources/brand-resources/> and its "YouTube Logo" / "YouTube Icon" sub-pages (including `/howyoutubeworks/resources/brand-resources/youtube-icon/`), fetched and additionally rendered with a headless browser (reviewed 14 Sep 2026) |
| Reason withheld | **No official compact asset could be retrieved, and the published guidance could not be read.** The brand-resources pages are client-rendered: the served HTML contains no asset URLs (no `.zip`/`.svg` references at all), the rendered DOM after hydration contains no download link either (assets are fetched by script at runtime), and the sub-page navigation resolves to the application shell rather than server-rendered guidance. Without the owner's asset and the owner's icon rules (permitted use, minimum size, clear space), the admission gate cannot be satisfied on items 1–3 and 5–7 of the brief. Substituting an icon file from elsewhere — including from YouTube's own product CDN — was rejected, because only artwork **designated by the owner as its official mark for the intended use** is admissible. |
| What would be needed before admission | The owner manually retrieves the official YouTube brand asset pack from the brand-resources page (interactive session), plus the published icon guidance (permitted "link to our channel"-style use, minimum size and clear space at digital sizes). The pack can then be re-reviewed and parked. |
| Notes | The Foundation ships the YouTube label as text, which is the correct production-safe fallback. |

## Out of scope for this set

Platforms the brief names as **future** connectivities (Signal, Discord, LINE,
WeChat, Microsoft Teams, Matrix, Bluesky, Threads, …) were deliberately **not**
researched or produced — they are not part of the initial production set. The
runtime's generic optional `icon` leaf means a future platform still requires no
engine change: it needs only an official asset and a review record like the ones
above.

## Consistency notes

* Every withhold above leaves the platform as an authoritative **text** link —
  already the runtime's correct behaviour; a withheld mark costs nothing at
  runtime and degrades gracefully (`connectivityIcon()` → `""` → no `<img>`,
  href and label intact).
* No withheld item was "approximated" with a lookalike, a monogram, a generic
  bubble glyph, or a third-party library icon. Where an official asset existed
  but the *use* was not clearly authorised (X, Viber), the asset was not parked.
* Withholds are expected to be revisited, not forgotten: each row names the
  exact contact, question and artefact that would unblock it.
