## How to read this file

Each admitted mark has a provenance record with the fields required by the
production brief: platform · canonical filename · official asset name · official
source owner · official source page · date reviewed · source format · source
dimensions/viewBox · SHA-256 of parked file · permitted intended-use basis ·
colour variant used · modifications performed · trademark/attribution
requirements · review notes.

**Admission rule applied without exception:** a mark was parked **only** when an
official source was identified, the exact intended mark was identified and
retrieved, the intended use (a small, labelled connectivity indicator) was
permitted or reasonably covered by that owner's **published** brand/trademark
rules, the required colour treatment was known, modification rules were known,
and the owner's own minimum-size / clear-space rules were compatible with the
runtime's **16 × 16 CSS px** slot. Anything unresolved was withheld
([`platform-marks-withheld.md`](platform-marks-withheld.md)) rather than guessed.

**No mark was redrawn, traced, approximated, recoloured, distorted, cropped,
outlined, animated, stored on a Foundation plate, or sourced from a third-party
icon library.** Every parked byte came out of a package published by the
platform owner on the owner's own domain, and the parked bytes are byte-identical
to the owner's file (verified by SHA-256 after parking).

## Method and source discipline (how these files were obtained)

For each platform the owner's own brand / trademark / press resource was located
first, and the **exact** intended mark was then retrieved from an artefact
published by that owner on the owner's own domain — e.g. `brand.github.com`,
`telegram.org`, `content.linkedin.com`, Meta's own CDN as linked from the Meta
Brand Resource Center, `about.x.com`. The parked files are byte-identical copies
of the owner's files (SHA-256 above was computed **from the parked bytes**, not
from the download).

Sources that were **never** used, for any mark: Font Awesome, Simple Icons,
Tabler, Wikipedia/Wikimedia, Iconify, logo-download or search-result sites, or
any community recreation. The existing universal icon inventory
(`universal-assets/icons/`, Tabler-derived) was **not** used as a substitute for
any platform mark, and the generic connectivity icons were not recreated.

Two owner-published *acknowledgment gates* were encountered and are recorded
rather than bypassed: the Meta Brand Resource Center packs are offered only after
"I have read and accept the applicable guidelines and other terms for use", and
the X brand toolkit is governed by the X Trademark Guidelines plus its Terms of
Service. Accepting those terms is an **owner** action. Retrieval of an official
artefact does not itself constitute acceptance or approval.

## Cross-cutting preconditions for the FUTURE integration phase

These are not defects in the parked files; they are conditions the runtime /
owner must resolve **before** any mark is copied into a deployment.

**P-1 — Surface/theme colour for monochrome marks (`github.svg`, `instagram.svg`).**
The runtime renders a connectivity mark from a single configured `icon`
filename, on a surface that follows the theme (`--background` is `#ffffff` in
light and `#0f172a` in dark). GitHub's and Instagram's parked variants are the
official **black** assets, which are correct on the light/white-dominant surface
and would be illegible on the dark surface. Before deployment either (a) the
connectivity row must render on a fixed light surface, or (b) the official
white variants must be swapped in for the dark surface, which the current
single-file `icon` leaf cannot express per theme. This is an
integration/engineering decision, not an artwork alteration — **do not**
recolour or filter the owner's files to work around it.

**P-2 — Facebook spacing illustration.** Facebook's published clear space (¼ of
the logo width = 4 px at 16 px) is satisfied by the shared rule's icon→label gap
(0.4 rem = 6.4 px), but the same page illustrates "at least half a logo distance"
(8 px) from other content. If the Facebook mark ships, the connectivity
icon→label gap should be raised to ≥ 0.5 em. That is a shared-CSS change and
deliberately **not** made in this parking pass.

**P-3 — Messenger's explicit linking sentence.** See §4 *Review notes*: the
Messenger page publishes the digital rendering rules (variant, ¼-width clear
space, 16 px minimum) but not a sentence as explicit as Facebook's "drive to your
presence". Confirmation from Meta is the remaining open item for that mark.

**P-4 — Account reality precedes artwork.** No accounts, handles, phone numbers
or profiles were invented anywhere in this pass, and none may be. A mark may only
be wired to a connectivity item that points at a **real, existing** Foundation
presence (and, for LinkedIn, that is the mark's own published condition).

## Integrity statement

```text
NO THIRD-PARTY MARK WAS REDRAWN, TRACED, RECOLOURED, DISTORTED, OR SOURCED FROM
AN UNOFFICIAL ICON LIBRARY.
```

Every parked byte is the platform owner's own published file, unmodified
(`modifications performed: NONE` in every record above). No Foundation plate,
circle, rounded square, background, border, shadow, gradient, outline, rotation,
crop or animation was added; no Foundation Blue was applied; no mark was
normalised into a custom house style. Any file whose official form was unsuitable
for the slot was **withheld** (see the companion register) or replaced by a
**different official variant from the same owner pack** — never repaired by hand.
Every SVG in the pack was additionally machine-checked after parking for zero
`<script>`, zero external `href`/`xlink:href`/`url()` references, zero embedded
`<image>`/base64 payloads and zero animation — all seven passed.

No page in this package claims the pack is complete: the twelve platforms in the
initial set were each reviewed independently, five were withheld, and the
directory deliberately contains fewer than twelve files.

## What this pass did NOT do (deliberate hard stops)

```text
no mark copied into any deployment's public/assets/
no config `icon` key added or changed
no account, handle, number, profile or presence invented or activated
no Foundation runtime, schema, component, CSS or test modified
no change to the living brand pack (brands-provelopment/…) or its social/ dirs
no change to the universal icon inventory or its provenance/licensing records
no platform-brand artwork drawn, approximated or "cleaned up"
```

Integration is a **later, separately-approved** phase: on MBA/owner approval the
files here are copied into the living pack's
`.../provelopment-foundation/social/platform-marks/` and then into the
deployment's `public/assets/`, with the connectivity item referencing that plain
filename through the existing generic optional `icon` leaf — no engine change per
platform, and the preconditions above resolved first.
